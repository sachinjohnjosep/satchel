# Updating your site

Everything is done from the dashboard at **`/admin`** on your site. It works the same on a phone and a laptop; no code, no redeploys.

**Tip for your phone:** open `/admin` in Safari or Chrome and use **Share → Add to Home Screen**. You stay signed in, so it behaves like an app.

## How publishing works

Every item (a piece of writing, a photograph, a film, a journal entry…) is either:

- **Draft**: only you can see it. Use **Preview** to see how it will look.
- **Published**: visible to everyone, and it appears on the home page and in its section straight away.
- **Scheduled**: published with a future date. It goes live within about five minutes of that time.

Buttons at the bottom of every editor (pinned to the bottom of the screen on a phone):

| You see | It does |
|---|---|
| **Save draft** / **Publish** | for something not yet live |
| **Save changes** / **Unpublish** | for something already live; Unpublish turns it back into a draft |

Pressing Enter in a field never publishes by accident: it saves a draft (or saves changes to something already live).

**How quickly do changes appear?** Immediately: saving refreshes the affected pages, the home page and the sitemap on the next visit. The only delay is for *scheduled* items (up to ~5 minutes after their time) and for anything cached in a visitor’s own browser.

If you try to leave a page with unsaved changes, the browser will warn you. If saving fails (say, your train goes into a tunnel), your text stays on screen; press save again.

## Writing

**Dashboard → Writing → New piece.**

- **Title** and optional **subtitle**.
- **Text**: the editor has bold, italic, two heading sizes, quotes, lists, links, dividers and images. For **poems**, use the **↵** button for a line break inside a stanza and Enter for a new stanza.
- **Excerpt**: one or two sentences for lists and the home page.
- **Category**: Essay, Poetry, Short story, Reflection, Observation, or your own. Poetry is shown without the large opening letter.
- **Tags**: separated by commas; existing ones are suggested.
- **Cover image**: optional, with a short description for screen readers.
- **Web address and sharing** (optional): the page address and how it appears in search results and when shared. Leave it empty and it is made from the title.

Reading time is worked out automatically. Related pieces are chosen from shared tags and category.

## Photographs

**Dashboard → Photographs → Add photograph.** Choose a photo (on a phone this opens your library or camera), then add a title and a **description** (what is in the picture, for people using screen readers).

- **Remove location and camera data** is ticked by default. Phone photos usually record exactly where they were taken, and this strips that before upload. Untick it only if you want the original file untouched.
- iPhone HEIC photos: if one is refused, set **Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible**, or share it as JPEG first.
- Photos are shown exactly as uploaded, with no overlays, at sizes optimised for each screen.
- **Category**, **tags**, **place** and **date taken** are all optional.

**Photo stories** (Dashboard → Photo stories) group photographs into a sequence with an introduction. Create the story, then open each photograph and choose the story under **Photo story**; **Order in story** sets the sequence.

## Sachinema (films)

**Dashboard → Films → Add film.** Title, year, director, your rating (0–5 in halves), a short review and, optionally, a longer reflection. Tick **One of my favourites** to add it to the Favourites row.

**Posters:** only upload images you’re allowed to use (a distributor’s press kit, or TMDB with credit) and fill in **Image credit**. Without a poster, the site shows a typographic title card, which looks deliberate, not missing.

**Film lists** (Dashboard → Film lists) are ordered lists like “Quiet films”. Add films and use Up/Down to order them.

## Listening (records and playlists)

**Dashboard → Listening → Add record.** Title, artist, year, kind (album, song, EP, live), your rating, a short note and optional longer thoughts. Tick **On repeat** to show it in the On repeat row and on the home page.

- **Listening link:** paste a link from Spotify, YouTube, Apple Music or SoundCloud (use the app's **Share → Copy link**). The page then shows a **Play** button; the actual player loads only when a visitor presses it, so nothing from those services tracks visitors before that. Bandcamp and other sites show a **Listen** button instead.
- **Cover:** optional. Upload only artwork you may use (for example your own photo of the sleeve). Without one, the site shows a coloured typographic sleeve.

**Dashboard → Playlists → New playlist.** Add records, order them with Up/Down, and optionally paste a link to the full playlist on Spotify or YouTube.

## Journal

**Dashboard → Journal → New entry.** Plain text, no title needed. Pick a kind: thought, observation, quotation (add the source) or update. A blank line starts a new paragraph.

## Notes from visitors

**Dashboard → Notes.** New notes are marked with a dot and counted in the menu. **Mark as read**, **Archive** (moves it out of the inbox) or **Delete** (permanent). Notes are never shown publicly and stay until you delete them.

## Home page, About and settings

**Dashboard → Settings**:

- **Site title, tagline, home page headline, accent colour** (oxblood, olive or faded red)
- **Introduction** and the **About page** text, **profile photo** and **social links** (one per line, `Label | https://…`)
- **Home page**: the featured photograph and pinned writing and films. Anything you don’t pin is filled with the most recent.
- **Search and sharing**: site description and default sharing image
- **Note notifications**: where to send emails (see SETUP.md, step 8)
- **Backup**: download everything as one file. Worth doing monthly.

## Replacing the sample content

Everything that came with the site is marked **Sample** on the site and in the dashboard, and the Overview counts how many are left. **Editing** a sample item makes it yours (the marker disappears); **delete** the ones you don’t need. The sample films are real films with placeholder reviews and ratings: rewrite or delete them.

## Searching and filtering in the dashboard

Every list has a search box and filters for **Drafts**, **Scheduled** and **Published**.
